# ⚡ VAJRA-X

###AI-Powered Thunderstorm & Lightning Nowcasting System

VAJRA-X is an AI/ML-based weather nowcasting platform designed to predict thunderstorms and lightning in the near future by combining multiple atmospheric data sources such as radar, satellite, lightning observations, atmospheric conditions, and weather model data.

The system aims to transform complex weather observations into easy-to-understand risk maps, storm movement predictions, and early warnings for disaster management.

🎯 Objective

The primary objective of VAJRA-X is to provide short-term, location-specific predictions of:

- 🌩️ Thunderstorm risk
- ⚡ Lightning risk
- 🌀 Storm movement and trajectory
- 📍 High-risk geographical zones
- 📊 Prediction confidence
- 🚨 Early warnings
